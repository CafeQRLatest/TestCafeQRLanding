Yes! Waiters can log into the CafeQR POS App on any Android smartphone or tablet to punch table orders, which automatically dispatches KOT slips to the kitchen printer instantly.

Yes. CafeQR supports all standard ESC/POS Bluetooth, USB, and WiFi thermal printers including 2-inch (58mm) and 3-inch (80mm) models like Hoin, Shreyans, Everycom, TVS, and EPSON.
